## Proposed resolution: Read-only account integration

The members/authorised committee of the above collective resolve:

1. To request from First National Bank (a division of FirstRand Bank
   Limited) access to the **Integration Channel Transaction History API**,
   or an authorised equivalent, solely to obtain account balances,
   dated transaction history and bank-origin references for member
   contribution reconciliation, administration and governance.
2. This resolution does **not** grant Khula's software, any service
   provider or administrator authority to initiate payments, approve
   withdrawals, change beneficiaries, create debit orders or trade.
3. The bank may provide access only to the account(s) designated by
   duly authorised signatories using the bank's authenticated
   consent/authorisation process. Consent may be revoked by the
   account holders through the bank's documented mechanisms.
4. The committee must complete its POPIA section 72 assessment of
   any overseas hosting and confirm operator agreements, access
   control, retention and incident response before enabling a live
   production connection.
5. The committee may appoint the following representative solely
   to liaise with FNB regarding the application and technical review:
   Name: ______________________________
   Role: ______________________________
   Verified contact email: ______________________________
6. The representative may **not** request or share online banking
   passwords, PINs, OTPs or nonconsented member financial records.
7. Before activation, the committee must review FNB's written
   eligibility decision, the bank's terms, intended read-only scopes,
   fees, risk assessment, approved data processing and test results.
8. This mandate expires or is superseded on: ________________________,
   and must be revoked when the app or club no longer needs access.
