# Khula Collective — real-member invitation policy

Only a named, active Khula administrator may generate a single-use token,
with a seven-day maximum lifetime and a hash stored in the ledger.
The invited email must match exactly; unused/expired/redeemed codes are
not accepted. Codes should be shared privately, never in a WhatsApp group.

## Before invitations can be issued in production

1. Record the club's real legal/association name, privacy contact,
   Information Officer registration, constitution approval and overseas
   data-transfer/operator safeguards with written evidence.
2. Verify that the person is an actual intended club member or requested an
   invitation. Do not use a scraped contact list or unconsented marketing.
3. Draft the invitation for the *specific* email in Member Invitations.
   Verify address and recipient manually; the system does not send on its own.
4. Tell recipients never to share bank login details, PINs or OTPs.
5. The recipient reads the privacy notice, acknowledges it, registers with
   the unique code and chooses a private password.
6. The account remains inactive until the administrator independently
   verifies membership eligibility and grants access. Do not claim that
   ID/checksum verification alone meets FICA, POPIA or banking compliance.
7. Maintain an access-audit trail, allow correction and handle member
   withdrawal requests in line with the constitution and retention policy.

## Template

**Subject:** Your private Khula Collective membership invitation

Hello,

You are invited to register for the Khula Collective members' portal:
https://khula-collective-production.up.railway.app

Use your registered email and the individual one-time code the
administrator provides privately. Read the privacy notice before
submitting your details.

An administrator must approve your membership after you register.
No bank PIN, online-banking password or OTP is ever required.

If you did not expect this email, disregard it and contact the
club administrator directly.

Khula Collective Administration

The in-app template includes the real single-use code and its expiry.
