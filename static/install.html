Install from the website

Khula in your pocket.

Put your private savings-club portal on your home screen. No app-store download required.

Get the Khula app

iPhone / iPad
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Enable Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add.
Android
  1. Open this page in Chrome or Samsung Internet.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen and confirm.
Install from your browser

Use your browser’s menu to choose Install app or Add to Home Screen. Browser and phone support may vary.

Open Khula Collective

You must still register and be approved by a club administrator before accessing private member information.

Khula is an installable web app, not an Android APK or an App Store download. An internet connection is required for sign-in and member data. It does not cache financial records for offline access.
